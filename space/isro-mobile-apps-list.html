Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Solar Calculator App developed by ISRO? +
The Solar Calculator App developed by ISRO/SAC provides monthly and yearly Global Horizontal Irradiance (GHI), Direct Normal Irradiance (DNI), and Diffused Horizontal Irradiance (DHI) using INSAT satellite data. It helps estimate solar energy potential and the optimal tilt angle for solar panels.
What navigation system powers the MapmyindiaNavIC App? +
The MapmyindiaNavIC App is powered by NavIC (Navigation with Indian Constellation), India's self-reliant regional satellite navigation system developed by ISRO, providing extremely precise positioning and navigation services.
How does the Nabhmitra App help fishermen? +
Developed for marine safety, Nabhmitra is a satellite-based communication device and app that provides two-way messaging, real-time weather advisories, cyclone alerts, and distress signaling for fishermen at deep sea.
What is the purpose of the Sagarmitra App? +
The Sagarmitra App provides ocean advisory services, including Potential Fishing Zone (PFZ) forecasts, wave height, ocean current warnings, and wind speed updates derived from Indian remote sensing satellites to assist maritime operations.
